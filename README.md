# Quantum Isomorphisms of Finite Graphs – Two Strongly Regular Graphs from the Penrose Dodecahedron

In this report we study a relation between graphs known as quantum isomorphism which come from the study of non-local games in quantum information and naturally generalize the notion of a graph isomorphism.
We give an overview of the few known examples of non-isomorphic quantum isomorphic graphs and aim to extend this list by analysing the orthogonality graph $Q$ of the Penrose dodecahedron and its clique graph $Q^∗$, both of which are strongly regular with parameters $(40,12,2,4)$ and have surprisingly many properties in common.
While we were neither able to prove nor to disprove the existence of a quantum isomorphism between $Q$ and $Q^∗$, we report several observations which strengthen our hypothesis and show how both graphs may be understood in terms of the 2-qutrit Pauli group.
